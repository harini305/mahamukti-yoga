Yoga teacher - Akhilesh Bodhi
Akhilesh Bodhi, the founder of Mahamukti Yoga, is a yoga instructor, Reiki master, and spiritual mentor with over 22 years of teaching experience. His journey in yoga began with a deep passion for ancient yogic traditions, which he has combined with modern practices to create a comprehensive approach to teaching.

His global teaching experience, from Bali to Europe and the United States, reflects his dedication to spreading the transformative power of yoga to diverse communities.
