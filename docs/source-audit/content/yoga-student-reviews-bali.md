If you are thinking about doing a YTTC, don't look further, you have found a hidden gem!

I have done yoga for almost 10 years, and Akhilesh is one of the best teachers I have got to know. I recommend anyone to take a class, workshop, retreat, or YTTC with him!
I have gotten to know Akhilesh as the teacher of my yoga teachers back in Delft, Netherlands at Neo Yoga. (I know, teacher of my teachers, very meta). At Neo Yoga, the founders and most of their teachers have been taught by Akhilesh. All of them are skilled and knowledgeable teachers. Even though they did their YTTC at the same place, they have their own style and energy. This underlines that at Maha Mukti they help you develop and find the unique yogi you have inside of you. Akhilesh does this with over 20 years of experience and personal attention. Which is becoming rare, now that yoga is so booming.
