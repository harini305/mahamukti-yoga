## Section 48: H3: Is Mahamukti Yoga Retreat Yoga Alliance certified?
<!-- elementor id=72d5374 top=6244px height=578px bg=#000000 @0 -->

Is Mahamukti Yoga Retreat Yoga Alliance certified?
Yes. All Yoga Teacher Training (YTT) courses at Mahamukti are registered with Yoga Alliance USA. Upon successful completion of the 200, 300, or 500-hour programs, you are eligible to become a Registered Yoga Teacher (RYT).
Where is the retreat located and how do I get there?
The retreat is located near Ubud, Bali. Most packages in 2026 include a one-way airport transfer from Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS), which is approximately a 1.5 to 2-hour drive depending on traffic.
Do I need to bring my own yoga mat?
No, Mahamukti provides high-quality yoga mats, blocks, and props at their open-air shala. However, many practitioners prefer to bring their own travel mat or a yoga towel for personal hygiene.
Can I attend if I have never done yoga before?
Absolutely. The 3-day and 6-day healing retreats are specifically designed to be beginner-friendly. Instructors provide modifications for every posture to ensure a safe and supportive experience for those new to the practice.
