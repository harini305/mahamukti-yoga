HATHA YOGA
#### Hatha Yoga
Do you know where all the yoga types originated? It was from Hatha yoga. It is like… the grandmother of all yoga styles! It includes all the basics of yoga – holding poses, breathing right, and finding your balance. It’s not as fast and intense as other yoga styles, which makes it perfect for beginners(people who started learning yoga). In this, you’ll learn how to align your body properly and build a strong foundation. Trust me, getting these basics right will make you a way better teacher!
In our Hatha classes, we’ll break down each pose in detail. You’ll learn:
- Proper alignment for safety and effectiveness
- How to use props to support different body types
- The benefits and contraindications of each pose
- How to sequence a well-balanced Hatha class
VINYASA YOGA
#### Vinyasa Yoga
Vinyasa is a little different from other yogas. It’s like a dance – moving from one pose to another, constantly matching your breath with your movement. Vinsaya yoga is more dynamic than Hatha yoga, and everyone loves it because it feels like a moving meditation.
We’ll teach you everything about vinsaya yoga, and will also guide you to keep your future students engaged. Sometimes it gets a bit sweaty, but that’s all part of the fun!
Our Vinyasa training includes:
- Creating seamless transitions between poses
- Building creative and balanced sequences
- Using music effectively in class
- Modifying flows for different levels
ASHTANGA YOGA
#### Ashtanga Yoga
Ashtanga yoga is a bit more intense than all other forms. It follows a specific sequence of poses and always in the same order. Many people find it a bit challenging, but don’t worry – we’ll teach you everything step by step. Learning Ashtanga will really boost your personal practice and give you tons of confidence as a teacher. Plus, it’s impressive to have on your yoga resume!
You’ll learn:
- The traditional Ashtanga sequence
- How to teach Mysore-style classes
- Proper vinyasa count and Sanskrit names
- Safe adjustment techniques
MEDITATION
#### Meditation
Meditation isn’t exactly a yoga style, but it’s SUPER important! We’ll teach you different meditation techniques. Some of it includes simple breathing exercises and mantras chantings. You might find it challenging to sit still at first (I know I did!), but by the end, you’ll be amazed at how much calmer and focused you feel.
We cover various meditation styles:
- Mindfulness meditation
- Loving-kindness meditation
- Mantra meditation
- Walking meditation
PRANAYAMA
#### Pranayama
This is entirely focused on managing your breath. Despite appearances, it is actually very impressive! We will demonstrate various breathing methods to enhance your energy, calm you down, or enhance your focus. At first, some practices might appear odd (like alternating single-nostril breathing), but as you grow accustomed to them, you will start to appreciate them. Your future students will also feel the same way!
You’ll learn pranayama techniques like:
- Ujjayi
- Nadi Shodhana
- Kapalabhati
- Bhramari

### Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need to be highly flexible to take part in the training?
Absolutely not! It would be amazing if you can reach your toes, but it’s not essential. We’ve had students of all age levels, who had completed the training successfully. What’s more important is your enthusiasm and willingness to learn!
How intense is this training?
I’m not going to sugarcoat it – it can be quite difficult for certain individuals! We begin our days bright and early and they are packed with activities. We gradually construct and there are always options for modifications. Pay attention to your body and take breaks when necessary – remember, it’s not a race!.
What's the food like? I'm vegetarian/vegan
Don’t worry! Our yoga school is super veggie-friendly. We provide healthy, mostly vegetarian meals with vegan options always available. The local fruits are amazing – you will get both healthy and tasty options here.
I'm not sure if I want to teach yoga. Should I still do the training?
In one word – Absolutely! Many people do the training just to deepen their own practice. Plus, I’ve also had several students who were adamant they wouldn’t teach, and now they’re running successful classes!
What's the accommodation like?
It’s comfortable but not luxurious. You’ll have everything you need, plus some amazing views. And trust me, after a full day of learning about yoga, nobody needs luxury!
What level of proficiency in yoga is required for participation?
No matter how much experience you possess, it’s not relevant. Your dedication to learning is the most crucial factor. Some newcomers who were highly committed have excelled in the course.
What if I struggle with the training?
It’s normal, it can happen to anyone! Our teachers and staff provide excellent support. We act like a large family while training, with everyone watching out for one another. Additionally, we offer wi-fi to ensure you can communicate with people back home.
What type of certification do you offer?
You’ll get a Yoga Alliance certified 200-hour certificate. It’s recognized pretty much everywhere, so you can teach internationally. But remember, the certificate is just the beginning – your real learning starts when you begin teaching!
